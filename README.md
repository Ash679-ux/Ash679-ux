**Projects**

- **[chem-ml-solubility](https://github.com/Ash679-ux/chem-ml-solubility)**: predicting how well molecules dissolve in water. A standard model got organophosphates (P=O compounds) badly wrong, probably because the P=O oxygen is a much stronger hydrogen-bond acceptor than the descriptors capture. Larger datasets and a P=O count cut their error by more than half.
- **[Molecule-Property-Explorer](https://github.com/Ash679-ux/Molecule-Property-Explorer)**: the follow-up, a web app that predicts solubility from a structure, explains the prediction, warns when a molecule is unlike the training data, and checks drug-likeness. [Live demo](https://molecule-explorer.streamlit.app) (it may take 30 seconds to wake up).
- **[swim-safety](https://github.com/Ash679-ux/swim-safety)**: is the river safe to swim in today? I built a dataset from five public sources (about 1,000 samples, 4 million rain and flow readings, 73,000 sewage spills) to predict bacteria failures at England's river bathing sites, and tested whether upstream sewage spills add risk beyond rain.

**Tools:** Python, RDKit, scikit-learn, pandas, Streamlit, pytest
